# Brain volume and dementia in older adults

**Status:** work in progress

## Question
How does brain volume relate to dementia status in adults aged 60–96?

## Data
OASIS longitudinal MRI dataset: 373 MRI visits from 150 adults, labelled
Nondemented, Demented or Converted. Downloaded from Kaggle.

Source: Marcus et al. (2010), "Open Access Series of Imaging Studies:
Longitudinal MRI data in nondemented and demented older adults",
*Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience*, 22(12), 2677–84.
